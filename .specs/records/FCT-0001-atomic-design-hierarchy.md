## Statement
User interfaces can be decomposed into five hierarchical compositional levels (Atoms, Molecules, Organisms, Templates, and Pages) where each level builds exclusively on lower levels or raw design tokens, preventing circular dependencies between foundational controls and page-level assemblies.
