## Statement
The dark obsidian canvas, translucent card surfaces, muted secondary text, and rose accent tokens adapted from Lumen will maintain readable WCAG AA contrast ratios across interactive controls, form inputs, and data cards outside a media-only catalog.

## How we validate it
Compute foreground-to-surface contrast ratios for every semantic token pair (primary text, secondary text, muted text, primary action, focus ring, and status badges) and inspect rendered Showcase Pages.
