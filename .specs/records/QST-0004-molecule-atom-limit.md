## Statement
A Molecule combines two or three kinds of Atom. The Callout, Code Block and Media Card Molecules already use three for their surface, icon and control or badge, and still show their own titles, text or code. Either the limit stays and these three keep that text themselves, or Atoms that only show text do not count towards it, or the three Molecules are split up.
